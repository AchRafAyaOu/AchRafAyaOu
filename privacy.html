This Privacy Policy explains how Vidia collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Vidia application and related services.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Vidia. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use Vidia, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

By using Vidia, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Vidia may collect or process certain information.

Information you provide

  • Account information, such as your name or email address.
  • Information you provide when contacting support.
  • Preferences or settings associated with your account.

Information collected automatically

  • Device type and operating system.
  • Application version.
  • Basic diagnostic and technical information.
  • Information necessary to maintain application security and reliability.

Vidia only collects information that is reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, improve, and secure its services.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide and operate Vidia.
  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To provide access to available content and features.
  • To maintain application security.
  • To detect and resolve technical problems.
  • To improve application performance and user experience.
  • To communicate with users when necessary.
  • To comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. Video Content

Vidia may provide access to video content through its application. The availability and type of content may vary depending on the version of the application and the services provided.

Vidia does not use the content you watch to identify you personally unless such processing is specifically required for a feature of the service.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or improve Vidia. Such providers may process information on our behalf and are expected to handle it in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal proceedings, or a valid governmental request.

6. Third-Party Services

Vidia may use third-party services to support application functionality, analytics, security, infrastructure, or other technical operations.

These services may process limited technical or other information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, resolve disputes, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, deletion, or information about how your data is processed.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the information provided below.

10. Children's Privacy

Vidia does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Vidia without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Vidia, our practices, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
